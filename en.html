What NRG does

Everything you need for refuelling — and nothing else.

Nearby search

Every station within your radius, coloured by price. The cheapest one wears a crown.

Plan a route

Enter start and destination and see every station along the way. You decide how far off the route they may be.

Alerts while driving

If you want, NRG tells you when a cheap station is coming up ahead.

Diesel, petrol, electricity

Diesel, Super E5 and E10 plus charging points across Europe, with power rating and connector type.

No data collection

No account, no ads, no analytics. Your settings stay on your device.

CarPlay

On iPhone, NRG shows the cheapest stations on your car’s screen as well.

Per-station prices from official sources. Charging points are available across Europe.
