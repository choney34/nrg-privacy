NRG – Support & Help

NRG shows you cheap fuel stations and charging points – near you and along your route. Here you'll find help and contact details.

Contact

Frequently asked questions

No fuel stations or prices are shown.

  • Check that you have allowed the app location access (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → NRG).
  • Price data is currently available for Germany, Austria, France, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Luxembourg, the United Kingdom and Mexico. In other countries no prices are shown; the app will let you know.
  • A data source may be temporarily unavailable – please try again a little later.

How does searching along a route work?

  • Switch to the route view and enter start and destination.
  • The corridor setting determines how far a station may be from the route.

Notifications while driving.

  • Turn on monitoring with the bell icon. For alerts in the background, the app needs “Always” location permission.
  • In Settings you can choose to receive notifications without sound.

Privacy

NRG collects no personal data and uses no tracking. Details: Privacy Policy.
